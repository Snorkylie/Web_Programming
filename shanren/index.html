01 THE GAME

THE JOURNEY BEGINS

Every step tells
a story.

Shanren is a 2D pixel-art action platformer with fantasy RPG and rogue-like ideas. Travel through different areas, take control of a lone adventurer and face the challenges ahead.

Explore the world by running and jumping, fight enemies, develop new abilities, and collect relics along the way.

My goal is to build and run Shanren in Unreal Engine, bringing the pixel-art world, character movement and combat together in a playable experience.
