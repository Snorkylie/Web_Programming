01 THE GAME

THE JOURNEY BEGINS

Every step tells
a story.

Shanren is a 2D pixel-art action platformer with fantasy RPG and rogue-like ideas. Travel through different areas, choose a class and face the challenges ahead.

Explore the world by running and jumping, fight enemies with magic or a blade, and collect relics along the way.

Do you prefer a sword empowered by magic, or the power of spells? Your journey begins with a choice.
